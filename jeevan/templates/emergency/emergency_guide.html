Basic First Aid Steps

Important Reminder

Always call emergency services (108) immediately before attempting first aid. These steps are meant to provide immediate assistance until professional help arrives.

  1. Assess the Situation

    Check if the scene is safe for you and the victim. Look for any immediate dangers like fire, electrical hazards, or traffic.

  2. Call Emergency Services

    Dial 108 immediately and provide clear information about the location and nature of the emergency.

  3. Check Responsiveness

    Gently tap the person's shoulder and ask loudly, "Are you okay?" If there's no response, the person is unconscious.

  4. Open the Airway

    Place one hand on the person's forehead and gently tilt the head back. Lift the chin with two fingers to open the airway.

  5. Check for Breathing

    Look, listen, and feel for breathing for no more than 10 seconds. If the person is not breathing normally, begin CPR.

  6. Control Bleeding

    Apply direct pressure to any bleeding wounds using a clean cloth or bandage. Elevate the injured area if possible.

  7. Keep the Person Comfortable

    Cover the person with a blanket to maintain body temperature. Stay with them until emergency services arrive.

Common Emergency Situations

Heart Attack
  • Chest pain or discomfort
  • Shortness of breath
  • Nausea or vomiting
  • Cold sweat
  • Pain in arm, neck, or jaw

Action: Call 108 immediately. Have the person sit down and rest. Give aspirin if available and not allergic.

Stroke
  • Sudden numbness or weakness
  • Confusion or trouble speaking
  • Severe headache
  • Vision problems
  • Loss of balance

Action: Call 108 immediately. Note the time symptoms started. Keep the person calm and comfortable.

Choking
  • Inability to speak or cough
  • Blue lips or face
  • Clutching at throat
  • Panic or distress

Action: Perform Heimlich maneuver. Call 108 if the person becomes unconscious. Continue CPR if needed.

Heat Stroke
  • High body temperature
  • Hot, dry skin
  • Rapid pulse
  • Confusion or unconsciousness
  • Nausea or vomiting

Action: Move to a cool place. Remove excess clothing. Apply cool water or ice packs. Call 108 immediately.

Hypothermia
  • Shivering
  • Slurred speech
  • Slow breathing
  • Weak pulse
  • Confusion

Action: Move to a warm place. Remove wet clothing. Cover with blankets. Call 108 if severe.

Electric Shock
  • Burns at entry/exit points
  • Muscle contractions
  • Unconsciousness
  • Irregular heartbeat
  • Difficulty breathing

Action: Turn off power source first. Call 108 immediately. Do not touch the person until power is off.

Prevention Tips

Home Safety
  • Keep emergency numbers visible
  • Install smoke detectors
  • Keep first aid kit accessible
  • Remove tripping hazards
  • Secure heavy furniture
Travel Safety
  • Carry emergency contact info
  • Know local emergency numbers
  • Keep medications accessible
  • Wear seatbelts
  • Don't drive under influence
Health Monitoring
  • Regular health checkups
  • Know your medical conditions
  • Keep medical records updated
  • Monitor vital signs
  • Take medications as prescribed

When to Call Emergency Services

Call 108 Immediately For:
  • Severe chest pain or pressure
  • Difficulty breathing or shortness of breath
  • Severe bleeding that won't stop
  • Unconsciousness or unresponsiveness
  • Signs of stroke (F.A.S.T. - Face drooping, Arm weakness, Speech difficulty, Time to call)
  • Severe allergic reactions
  • Suicidal thoughts or self-harm
  • Any life-threatening situation
